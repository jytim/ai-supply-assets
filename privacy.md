# Privacy Policy / 隱私政策

Last updated: 2026-11-13

## English

This app is an internal tool used only to run the Instagram and Threads account **@ai.supply.tw** (學生 AI 補給站). It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

**What the app does**

- Publishes posts to the @ai.supply.tw account.
- Reads that account's own posts, the comments under them, and their performance numbers (such as reach, views, likes, saves and shares).
- Replies to comments under that account's own posts.

**Data it handles**

- Content and statistics of the @ai.supply.tw account itself.
- Public comments that other people leave under that account's posts, including the commenter's public username and the comment text, as provided by the Meta APIs.

The app does not collect contact details, does not build profiles of individuals, does not use the data for advertising, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

**Storage and retention**

Performance numbers are kept as counts without personal information. Comments are kept only as long as needed to answer them and to keep a record of what the account replied. Access tokens are stored in the operator's protected credential store and are never published.

**Deleting your data**

You can delete your own comment on Instagram or Threads at any time. To ask for any stored copy to be removed, send a direct message to **@ai.supply.tw** on Instagram or Threads. Requests are handled within 30 days.

**Contact**

Direct message **@ai.supply.tw** on Instagram or Threads.

## 中文

這個應用程式是內部工具，只用來經營 Instagram 與 Threads 帳號 **@ai.supply.tw**（學生 AI 補給站）。不對外開放，其他人無法登入使用。

**它做什麼**

- 在 @ai.supply.tw 帳號發布貼文。
- 讀取這個帳號自己的貼文、貼文底下的留言，以及成效數字（例如觸及、瀏覽、讚、收藏、分享）。
- 回覆這個帳號自己貼文底下的留言。

**它處理的資料**

- @ai.supply.tw 帳號本身的內容與統計數字。
- 其他人在這個帳號貼文底下公開留下的留言，包含留言者的公開用戶名稱與留言文字，由 Meta 的 API 提供。

不蒐集聯絡方式，不建立個人檔案，不用於廣告，不出售也不提供給第三方。

**保存方式與期間**

成效數字只保存數量，不含個人資訊。留言只在回覆所需的期間保存，並留下帳號回覆了什麼的紀錄。存取權杖放在經營者受保護的憑證儲存區，不會公開。

**刪除你的資料**

你可以隨時在 Instagram 或 Threads 刪除自己的留言。若要求移除已保存的副本，請在 Instagram 或 Threads 私訊 **@ai.supply.tw**，會在 30 天內處理。

**聯絡方式**

在 Instagram 或 Threads 私訊 **@ai.supply.tw**。
